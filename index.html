10 Golden Rules for Safe Online Shopping

1. Spot Unrealistic Discounts

If a brand new ₹80,000 iPhone is listed for ₹5,000, it is 99.9% guaranteed to be a counterfeit or payment scam.

2. Audit Seller History

Check seller registration date and rating count. Brand new accounts with zero established feedback are high risk.

3. Inspect Web Domain (URL)

Verify the URL spelling carefully. Watch out for typosquatting like amaz0n-cheap-deals.site instead of official domains.

4. Detect Bot Reviews

Watch for dozens of generic 5-star reviews posted on the exact same date using identical phrases like "Great item original fast delivery".

5. Never Pay Off-Platform

Never accept requests to wire direct bank transfers, UPI links, or crypto for marketplace purchases outside escrow protection.

6. Verify Return & Warranty

Always read the fine print. Genuine retailers state explicit 7-14 day return periods and manufacturer warranty coverage.

7. Beware Urgency Language

Scam listings frequently use artificial timers, CAPSLOCK text, and "ONLY 2 LEFT ACT NOW" pressure to prevent critical thinking.

8. Choose Cash on Delivery

Where available on unfamiliar stores, select Cash on Delivery (COD) or pay via credit card with buyer protection chargeback rights.

About FakeBazaar AI

FakeBazaar AI is a client-side cybersecurity tool engineered to help consumers evaluate online product listings, marketplace seller behavior, and domain characteristics before spending money.

Privacy & Zero-Server Guarantee

This application runs 100% locally in your web browser. No product details, URLs, or personal data are ever transmitted to any remote server or external database. All scan history is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage.

Methodology & Limitations

Risk scores are calculated using a weighted multi-factor heuristic evaluation algorithm covering pricing variance, seller rating distribution, review density, listing linguistic markers, and URL structural indicators. It provides probabilistic risk estimation and does not serve as legal proof of counterfeit or legitimate product status.
